## Rooms · The gala

### Showroom
The big hall, under the chandeliers.
- **Who:** Mabel Quist
- **Things:**
  1. **A brass umbrella stand**, too heavy to tip.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: one umbrella is dry, though it is raining outside.
     - 🎲 GM-ONLY-roll: 4/5: a cloakroom ticket in its folds. 6: and the number on it is circled.
     - ! GM-ONLY-danger: the stand rings like a bell if it is knocked.
     - State: full · empty
     - Tags: clue
  2. **A velvet rope** on two brass posts.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: one post is hollow and unscrews at the knob.
     - Tags: cover
  3. **The stage curtain**, heavy with dust.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: a door behind it, painted to look like the wall.
     - 🎲 GM-ONLY-roll: 4/5: it opens on the wings. 6: and on the stairs to the dressing rooms.
     - State: shut · open
     - Tags: escape, cover
  4. **A grand piano**, its lid propped open.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: sheet music with a name pencilled in the margin.
     - Tags: talk, clue
- **Costume:** Evening dress and white gloves
- If it goes wrong: the band stops mid-bar
