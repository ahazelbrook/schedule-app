# Pelican Rooms: invented fixture pages

*Invented for the room key build's tests. Nothing here is from a campaign.*

## Pelican bar
- **Band:** public
- **Say:**
  1. A long bar in a low room, the floor still wet from the mop. Somebody has left the radio on.
  2. Brass rails, green glass and one clean stool. The band next door thumps through the wall.
  3. The bar is quiet for a Friday. Every bottle has been turned to face the door.
- **People:** waiter 3 · cleaner 1 · nobody 2
- **Things:**
  1. **A long zinc bar**, Wet rings on the zinc, a cloth left folded.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: a till key taped under the lip, still warm.
     - 🎲 GM-ONLY-roll: 4/5 a few notes. 6: the whole float.
     - State: closed · open
     - Tags: loot
  2. **A row of upturned stools**, Legs in the air, ready for the floor to be swept.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: one stool is bolted down and rattles.
     - 🎲 GM-ONLY-roll: 4/5 a bolt, loose. 6: a stool leg that makes a club.
     - Tags: weapon, cover
  3. **A mirror behind the bottles**, It shows the door better than the door does.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: a smear where someone wiped a name off the glass.
     - Tags: clue
  4. **A glass-washer, humming**, Warm steam leaks from the lid.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: a wristwatch in the rinse tray.
     - 🎲 GM-ONLY-roll: 4/5 the watch is stopped. 6: it is engraved.
     - ! GM-ONLY-danger: The lid scalds.
     - State: shut · open
     - Tags: clue, hazard
  5. **A service hatch**, A square of dark at knee height, hinged.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: it opens on the kitchen passage.
     - State: shut · open
     - Tags: escape, door
  6. **A waiter’s tray**, Left on the bar with one clean glass.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: a folded note under the glass, a table number.
     - Tags: clue, talk
  7. **A jar of cocktail sticks**, Coloured, with little paper flags.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: one flag is a tight scroll of paper.
     - 🎲 GM-ONLY-roll: 4/5 a time. 6: a time and a street.
     - Tags: tool
  8. **A velvet rope on brass posts**, It cuts the bar off from the dance floor.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: one post is hollow and rings.
     - ! GM-ONLY-danger: The posts fall like dominoes.
     - Tags: cover, hazard
  9. **A radio on the shelf**, A dance band, turned low.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: it cuts out when anyone nears the till.
     - State: on · off
     - Tags: weird
  10. **A barman’s apron on a hook**, Stiff with old cordial.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: the pocket holds a pencil and a rota.
     - Tags: costume

## Pelican cloakroom
- **Band:** working
- **Say:**
  1. A narrow cloakroom that smells of rain and hair oil. Numbered tags clink on the rail.
  2. Coats packed so close that the room muffles every word. A bell sits on the counter.
  3. Cream walls and a single lamp. The attendant’s stool is empty and still warm.
- **People:** cleaner 1 · nobody 4
- **Things:**
  1. **A rail of coats**, Numbered tags swing on every hanger.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: one coat is far too heavy for its owner.
     - 🎲 GM-ONLY-roll: 4/5 a purse. 6: a purse and a key.
     - Tags: loot, cover
  2. **A brass bell on the counter**, Dented, with a ribbon for the hammer.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: the ribbon is cut, not frayed.
     - ! GM-ONLY-danger: It rings the whole house.
     - Tags: hazard
  3. **A ticket book**, Pink stubs, torn along the dotted line.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: the last stub has two names on it.
     - Tags: clue
  4. **A hat shelf**, Fedoras and one feathered thing.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: a hat is dry on a wet night.
     - Tags: costume
  5. **A narrow side door**, Painted the same cream as the wall.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: it opens on the alley, and its bolt is oiled.
     - State: bolted · open
     - Tags: escape, door
  6. **An umbrella stand**, A dozen black umbrellas, one very long.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: the long one is a blade in a sleeve.
     - 🎲 GM-ONLY-roll: 4/5 a blade, short. 6: a blade, and a lord’s crest.
     - Tags: weapon
  7. **A lost-property box**, Gloves, a spectacle case, a child’s shoe.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: a diary with the corner turned down.
     - Tags: clue, loot
  8. **A curtain on a pole**, It hides the back of the room.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: a person could stand behind it, and someone has.
     - Tags: cover
  9. **A stool for the attendant**, Low, with a cushion worn flat.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: a thermos, and the lid is still hot.
     - Tags: talk
  10. **A flickering lamp**, A shade the colour of weak tea.
     - 🔍 GM-ONLY-look: it flickers when the lift runs.
     - State: on · off
     - Tags: weird, tool
